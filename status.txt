If both the positive and negative CSA outputs are divided by the same ratio, the differential signal is also divided by that ratio — no factor of 2 there.

Looking at the schematic: the ADC pin connects to the junction of R1/R7 (VSENA) — that's single-ended. The full differential would be VSENA - midpoint, but the midpoint reference isn't being subtracted in hardware.
